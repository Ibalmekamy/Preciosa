MI AMOR

Para mi amada

De nadie más que de un extraño vagabundo que solo intenta explicar lo que siente por ti.

I hate the way she talks to me until 3 AM making me forget that the rest of the world even exists. I hate her evil laugh on voice notes because it sounds so cute. I hate how she acts so naive and innocent when we both know she is the smartest person in the room and I hate that she thinks she can hide from me. I hate the way she laughs and makes fun of her own problems just to protect me from the weight because I hate that she thinks she has to be a hero all the time. I hate the way she ships me with others or pretends it is all a joke just because she is too scared to admit what is actually happening between us. I hate the way she calls me her favorite person because in that split second I actually feel like I am truly living and I hate how much power that gives her. I hate the way she says she misses me because it sounds so rare that it makes everything else feel like a lie. I hate that I do not want something that fades and I want the fire she carries even if it burns me. I hate that I cannot even find a way to actually hate her not even a little bit not even at all. But mostly I hate the way I do not want anyone else in this world except for her.
